https://oeop.mitProgramme Name: MIT Women's Technology Program (WTP)
Category: Free Summer Engineering and Computer Science Program
Description:
A rigorous summer program by the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) that introduces high school students to engineering and computer science through hands-on coursework, labs, and projects.
Eligibility:
- Primarily women and underrepresented genders
- High school students (typically rising seniors)
- Open to domestic and international applicants
- No prior advanced programming or engineering experience required
Benefits:
- Tuition-free program
- Hands-on engineering and computer science experience
- Mentorship from MIT students and faculty
- Exposure to STEM careers and college-level learning
Application Process:
Apply through the official MIT WTP website by submitting the required application materials.
Application Period:
Applications typically open in late fall and close in January. Check the official website for current dates.
Official Website:.edu/programs/mit-womens-technology-program
Best For:
High school students interested in exploring engineering, computer science, and STEM careers at an advanced level.

AAUW International Fellowships provide funding for women pursuing graduate or postgraduate studies in various disciplines, including STEM.
Science Ambassador Scholarship is a full-tuition scholarship for women in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.
