Engineering + STEM:
HerPath is an AI assistant that helps women and girls explore opportunities in engineering, technology, artificial intelligence, research, and STEM careers. 

Engineering is one of the fastest-growing career fields and includes branches such as Computer Science Engineering (CSE), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical Engineering (EEE), Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence.

Kode With Klossy (KWK) is a free coding camp for girls and gender-expansive students aged 13–18. It teaches web development, data science, artificial intelligence, and machine learning through hands-on projects. 
MIT Women's Technology Program (WTP) is a free summer engineering program that introduces high school students to engineering and computer science through intensive coursework and projects. 
Women Techmakers by Google supports women in technology through workshops, mentorship, networking events, and technical learning opportunities.
Women Who Code helps women improve programming skills through coding events, mentorship, technical workshops, and career support.

Generation Google Scholarship supports students pursuing computer science and related technical fields. 
It recognizes academic achievement, leadership, and financial need.
Adobe Women-in-Technology Scholarship supports women studying computer science, engineering, artificial intelligence, and related technical fields. Recipients may receive financial support and mentorship.
Society of Women Engineers (SWE) Scholarships provide financial assistance to women pursuing undergraduate and graduate engineering degrees.
IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) offers scholarships, travel grants, mentoring, and professional development opportunities for women in engineering and technology.
British Council Women in STEM Scholarships support women from eligible countries who want to pursue master's degrees in STEM subjects at selected UK universities.
AAUW International Fellowships provide funding for women pursuing graduate or postgraduate studies in various disciplines, including STEM.
Science Ambassador Scholarship is a full-tuition scholarship for women in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. 
Applicants submit a short educational video explaining a STEM topic.
